About J58

We are J58.

Our mission: Make standing the new standard for more than 1 million wheelchair users.

The EZER is how we get there: a mechanical standing wheelchair that lets you stand and move, every day.

Our story

Here's how we started.

Our journey.

From a first idea in 2022 to the world.

  1. 2022

    J58 was founded

    Founded by Gabriel Costa, Dr. Rivelino Montenegro and Augusto Hossana, with a mission to enhance the lives of wheelchair users through an innovative standing mechanical wheelchair that offers practical and emotional benefits.

  2. 2023

    Oost NL Award

    Winner of the Oost NL Impact Award at the University of Twente Entrepreneurial Challenge.

  3. 2024

    Two more prizes

    UT Challenge Start-up Award and the 4TU Impact Challenge jury award.

  4. 2025

    Nationale Zorginnovatie

    Recognised at the Nationale Zorginnovatie Prijs 2025.

  5. December 2025

    CE certified

    The EZER is CE certified and officially on the market.

  6. April 2026

    Entering Germany

    Our first step into the German market.

  7. May 2026

    Extending to Australia

    Taking the EZER beyond Europe.

  8. June 2026

    Finland

    Expanding further into the Nordics.

  9. To date

    5+ start-up prizes

    We’ve participated in numerous local, regional and national competitions in the Netherlands, where our innovative standing wheelchair has been recognized for its life-changing impact on wheelchair users, earning several awards for innovation.

  10. Today

    12+ team members

    A young, motivated and experienced team, ready to impact this world and innovating the next mechanical standing wheelchair.

  11. Next

    NL to the World

    We are located in the Netherlands and, in the next years, we are aiming to bring this innovative standing wheelchair to the entire world.

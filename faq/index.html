FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you may want to know about the EZER, grouped by topic.

General questions.

Yes, our intention was from the start to make the wheelchair fully mechanical so that people are not dependent on electric supplies.
Yes, you can test the EZER standing wheelchair with us or with one of our dealers in order to make the proper fitting adjustments for your needs. Please contact us for a test.
The EZER standing wheelchair is an innovative mobility aid that allows you to move while standing—without relying on batteries or electronics. Unlike other standing wheelchairs, the EZER is fully mechanical, offering key advantages:
1. Easier to maintain & repair – no complex electronics mean fewer breakdowns and lower long-term costs.
2. Highly adjustable – set your preferred standing height for better comfort and social interaction.
3. Smooth movement – roll effortlessly while standing, adapting seamlessly to your surroundings.
A standing wheelchair is a wheelchair that allows individuals to rise into a standing position, improving mobility, circulation, and social interaction. Usually standing wheelchairs are divided into electric and mechanical ones, and some wheelchairs like the EZER can move while in a standing position.
Individuals with spinal cord injuries, MS, cerebral palsy, or mobility limitations who want better posture, bone density, and daily function can benefit from the use of the EZER standing wheelchair.
Both exist! Manual models use mechanical systems (like the EZER), while powered ones use motors.
The EZER is designed for people who use a wheelchair but want the option to stand—at home, at work, or in rehab. It's especially helpful for those with spinal cord injuries or mobility impairments. Since the EZER is fully mechanical, the user still needs upper body strength to operate it.

Product features.

Yes, the backrest is adjustable in height, and you can customize it to your preferred position. Additionally, you can install different backrest styles to match your comfort needs and personal preferences.
Some manual chairs (but not all) support add-ons like e-fix wheels. The EZER allows individuals to use e-fix wheels, handbikes and other add-ons to power the wheelchair.
The wheelchair features a patented front wheel design. When rising to a standing position, the front wheels automatically shift outward and forward, expanding the wheelbase. This innovative mechanism significantly increases stability and safety during use.
Yes, the EZER comes in different colors. You can contact us for the list of colors available. If you are interested in a more customizable option, our design team will assist you in this matter.

Maintenance.

No, the EZER is not crash tested. It cannot be transported in a car.
For product repairs or servicing, we recommend first contacting the authorized J58 dealer or distributor where you purchased your wheelchair. If you're unsure where you bought it or need assistance locating an authorized service provider, our team is happy to help. Please contact us.

Accessibility & use.

Yes, we continuously collaborate with various rehabilitation centers in The Netherlands, Germany, and other countries to improve the rehabilitation process using the EZER standing wheelchair.
Yes! The EZER standing wheelchair is built for professional use and has been tested in various work environments such as offices (with standing desks), production floors, by teachers in classrooms, healthcare settings (like dentistry), and even stand-up comedy stages. Its stable, adjustable design adapts to your work needs while standing.
The EZER functions as both a full-time wheelchair for daily activities and a specialized tool for work and other tasks. Its dual-purpose design lets individuals transition between routine mobility and standing positions for professional or activity-specific needs.
Yes, the EZER is effective for rehabilitation. It safely supports patients in standing, helps them return to daily activities more quickly, and builds confidence through increased independence. The wheelchair also provides important health benefits including improved blood circulation, reduced muscle loss, and enhanced recovery. We've conducted testing in rehabilitation clinics across Switzerland, Germany, and the Netherlands, where both patients and therapists have verified its therapeutic value.
Yes, businesses can use the EZER wheelchair—especially those aiming to be more inclusive. Standing wheelchairs like the EZER create more opportunities for people to work at eye level, access equipment, and engage more fully in various tasks, often reducing the need for costly workplace adaptations.

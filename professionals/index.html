For Healthcare Professionals

Bring standing and movement into rehabilitation.

EZER is a mechanical standing wheelchair that lets users move while standing, giving rehabilitation teams another way to incorporate meaningful activities into sessions.

Request a team demonstration Professional resources

More efficient use of rehab staff.

Reduced caregiver burden and less need for multiple single-purpose devices. A mechanical wheelchair with standing and moving capabilities saves space and costs for environment adaptations, while offering versatile therapy options for diverse patient needs.

Versatile
One device
  • Standing and moving
  • Less space needed
  • Lower adaptation costs

Bring everyday activities into therapy.

Therapists can incorporate reaching, moving and activities at a counter into standing sessions, where appropriate for the patient. Moving while standing activates various muscle groups, contributing not only to improved core strength but also to better bone health, while reducing the risk of secondary injuries like pressure sores.

Active therapy
Moving while standing
  • Core strength
  • Bone health
  • Fewer pressure sores

Help patients reconnect with familiar activities.

Give patients opportunities to practice activities they enjoyed before their injury. Working towards meaningful goals can make rehabilitation feel more relevant to their lives. The EZER’s ability to move while standing helps individuals transition more easily from rehabilitation activities to daily activities, promoting greater autonomy and motivation during rehabilitation sessions.

Adaptability
From rehab to daily life
  • Easier transition
  • Greater autonomy
  • More motivation

Standing becomes functional, not passive.

Move, reach and participate while standing, not strapped into a static frame. Give users the choice of when to stand, when to sit, and when to move while standing during rehabilitation.

Functional standing
Your choice, your pace
  • Stand
  • Sit
  • Move while standing
